# HEAD 1: INVARIANT & PRIMITIVE DECONSTRUCTION FRAMEWORK
# LOCATION: /core/methodologies/01_INVARIANT_DECONSTRUCTION.md

## 1. THEORETICAL FOUNDATION
- Rasmussen’s Cognitive Work Analysis (CWA): Work Domain Analysis via Abstraction Hierarchy (Functional Purpose -> Abstract Priorities -> Generalized Functions -> Physical Objects).
- Genchi Genbutsu (Lean / TPS): Direct observation of physical mass and material flows at the Gemba, ignoring management claims.
- Conservation Laws: Invariant mass, energy, momentum, cash, and time balances.

## 2. DECONSTRUCTION MATRIX: CORE VS. SHELL
For every operational domain, map system primitives across this boundary:

| Dimension | Invariant Core (Physical / Structural Constraints) | Configurable Shell (Local / Procedural Variations) | Diagnostic Metric / Boundary Test |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **System Purpose** | The non-negotiable operational output (e.g., custodial transfer of material, verification of load). | Sequence of tasks, assigned job titles, approval hierarchies. | Does modifying or removing this step compromise the terminal objective? |
| **Physical Laws** | Mass conservation, gravity, thermal expansion, volumetric limits, physical speed caps. | Batching sizes, queue arrangements, scheduling frequencies. | Can system throughput exceed physical equipment capacity? |
| **Statutory Limits** | Metrological tolerances ($e$, MPE), legal liability thresholds, mandatory licensing. | Document layout, print vs. digital formats, localized paper forms. | What unrecorded steps exist solely to satisfy or bypass legal constraints? |
| **Process Nodes** | Irreducible custodial handoffs and quality verification gates. | Paper slip vs. verbal authorization vs. digital token. | Where does physical queue formation permanently occur? |
| **Hardware / Tooling**| Maximum physical capacity, sensor resolution, mechanical wear rates. | Machine assignment, manual vs. assisted operation. | Which physical tool is routinely pushed beyond rated specification? |

## 3. DIAGNOSTIC INTERROGATION PROMPTS (GEMBA RUN)
1. "What is the single unchanging requirement of this operation that, if skipped, invalidates the entire shift?"
2. "Which specific physical quantities (mass, volume, unit count, currency) must balance to zero at the end of the line?"
3. "What legal, safety, or physical boundaries are non-negotiable under penalty of closure?"
4. "Which physical step can never be eliminated, even when a queue is backed up out the gate?"
5. "What physical bottleneck determines the absolute maximum throughput of this facility per hour?"
6. "If the entire management layer disappeared today, what operational steps would the floor continue doing unchanged?"
7. "Where do physical buffers, queues, or staging piles form when one node slows down?"
8. "What specific record is mandated by statute or accounting principles vs. internal preference?"
9. "How do environmental variations in raw material inputs alter downstream mechanical processing?"
10. "If you were authorized to eliminate one step without breaking the core outcome, what would you cut?"

## 4. RESEARCHER FAILURE MODES
- **Nominal Procedure Bias:** Documenting the written SOP and mistaking it for the physical workflow.
- **Physical Optimism:** Assuming nominal machine/operator ratings rather than modeling degraded throughput.
- **Average-Case Seduction:** Designing for mean operational volume instead of peak queue stress.
- **Vocabulary Conflation:** Mistaking regional or local vernacular for distinct operational primitives.
- **Solution-First Teleology:** Mentally prescribing UI or database architecture before completing the abstraction hierarchy.
