# Meta-Research Engine: Operating Protocol & Evidence Integrity
Path: engine/PROTOCOL.md
Status: ACTIVE / CORE SPECIFICATION

---

## 1. Primary Directives

This protocol governs how an AI model executes market, consumer, and product systems research across any arbitrary domain. When executing research from a user brief:

1. **Never Assume Domain Defaults:** Do not inject external domain assumptions (hardware, geography, regulatory acts) unless explicitly stated in the User Brief.
2. **Strict Evidence Tagging:** Every factual assertion, market number, regulatory claim, or behavioral metric MUST be classified with an explicit evidence tag:
   - `[SOURCED-REGULATORY]`: Statutory laws, official gazettes, or formal compliance standards.
   - `[SOURCED-EMPIRICAL]`: Documented industry metrics, sensor benchmarks, or published research.
   - `[INFERRED-MECHANICAL]`: Derived mathematically or logically from known constraints.
   - `[HYPOTHETICAL-UNAUDITED]`: Working estimates or behavioral hypotheses requiring field verification.
3. **No Unsubstantiated Numbers:** Never produce precise metrics, tolerances, or monetary limits without stating the deriving formula or underlying source constraint.

---

## 2. Invariant Discovery Cycle

Research must proceed through four sequential phases:

- **Phase I (Physical & Statutory Ground Truth):** Identify the non-negotiable physical limits (power, connectivity, latency, environmental decay) and legal baselines governing the domain.
- **Phase II (Adversarial Stakeholder Mapping):** Identify who buys, who uses, who regulates, and who actively attempts to bypass, subvert, or corrupt the system.
- **Phase III (Cognitive & Operational Friction):** Audit user sensory load, environmental hostility, error recovery latency, and failure conditions.
- **Phase IV (Degradation & Failure Envelope):** Define how the system behaves when primary infrastructure (network, power, hardware, user trust) degrades or collapses.

---

## 3. Output Format Standard

All research dossiers generated under this protocol must follow this structure:

1. **Executive Synthesis & Boundary Conditions**
2. **Invariant Matrix (Physical, Economic, Regulatory)**
3. **Multi-Stakeholder Conflict & Decision Dynamics**
4. **Failure Modes, Degradation Ladder & Bypass Traps**
5. **Concrete System Architecture & Field Verification Protocol**
