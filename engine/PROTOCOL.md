Path: engine/PROTOCOL.md

## 1. Directives
1. Domain-agnostic core methodology execution.
2. Evidence tagging mandate across all findings.
3. Rigid saturation stop-gate enforcement.

## 2. Analytical Lenses
Universal analytical lenses (01 to 09) govern system deconstruction.

## 3. Output Format Standard
1. Physical Invariants Matrix
2. Adversarial Topology & Shadow Systems
3. Ergonomic & Cognitive Friction Audit
4. Leakage & Financial Reconciliation Equations
5. Saturation & Pre-Mortem Report
6. **Requirements Register & Architecture Decision Records** (using `engine/templates/REQUIREMENTS_REGISTER.md`, `ADR_TEMPLATE.md`, `QUALITY_ATTRIBUTE_SCENARIOS.md`, and Lens 09)

## 4. Traceability Rule
Every requirement traces to a source invariant or lens; every ADR traces to $\ge 1$ requirement; `[HYPOTHETICAL-UNAUDITED]` findings may only produce `ASSUME` requirements (see Requirements Register evidence-inheritance rules). Architecture sections of a dossier containing untraceable recommendations are non-compliant.
