# HEAD 2: ADVERSARIAL ETHNOGRAPHY & SHADOW-SYSTEM MAPPING
# LOCATION: /core/methodologies/02_ADVERSARIAL_ETHNOGRAPHY.md

## 1. THEORETICAL FOUNDATION
- Hollnagel’s Functional Resonance Analysis Method (FRAM): Work-as-Imagined (WAI) vs. Work-as-Done (WAD).
- Cressey’s Fraud Triangle: Operational Pressure, Structural Opportunity, and Personal Rationalization.
- Klein’s Critical Decision Method (CDM): Probing non-linear decision points and tacit knowledge under stress.

## 2. SHADOW-SYSTEM RECONCILIATION MATRIX

| Operational Aspect | Work-as-Imagined (Official SOP) | Work-as-Done (Observed Reality) | Forensic Discrepancy Indicator |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Execution Flow** | Step 1 -> Step 2 -> Step 3 sequentially. | Steps re-ordered, skipped, or executed in bulk at shift end. | Timestamps clustered in batches; unrecorded physical handoffs. |
| **Data Capture** | Primary official software / central register. | Pocket diaries, chalk tallies, loose paper chits, WhatsApp text. | Mismatch between physical state and digital log entries. |
| **Settlement** | Immediate, formal digital/bank transaction. | Cash advances, informal IOUs, off-the-books adjustments. | Discrepancies between cash-in-drawer and system totals. |
| **Communication** | Formal reports, logged tickets, structured logs. | Verbal confirmations, informal hand gestures, phone calls. | Discrepancies resolved without a corresponding paper trail. |
| **Role Enforcement**| Strict segregation of duties (operator vs. auditor). | Fluid task sharing, helpers operating primary controls. | Unregistered individuals handling measurement or settlement. |

## 3. ADVERSARIAL FIELD PROMPTS
1. "What exact steps are dropped or handled differently when supervisors or owners leave the floor?"
2. "When the queue doubles and time pressure spikes, where do operators take shortcuts?"
3. "Who typically challenges an operational measurement, and what informal negotiation follows?"
4. "Where do personal pocket notebooks, chalk tallies, or rough chits exist outside the formal ledger?"
5. "How does physical material or cash leave this facility without appearing on an official register?"
6. "Who loses cash, authority, or informal revenue if this operation becomes completely transparent?"
7. "What undocumented payments, allowances, or 'speed fees' change hands during this process?"
8. "How do workers and counterparties assist one another by informally bypassing formal rules?"
9. "What daily operational practice would cause a shutdown or firing if corporate leadership observed it?"
10. "When a mechanical or measurement failure occurs, how is the downtime hidden or smoothed over?"

## 4. RESEARCHER FAILURE MODES
- **The Hawthorne Effect:** Believing observed worker compliance during an announced site visit.
- **Naive Realism:** Accepting early interviewee statements without forensic corroboration.
- **Institutional Alignment:** Conducting interviews exclusively with management or compliant senior staff.
- **PR Seduction:** Documenting corporate-approved explanations for operational variances.
- **Inquiry Dilution:** Softening adversarial questions regarding theft, bribery, and bypasses out of social discomfort.
