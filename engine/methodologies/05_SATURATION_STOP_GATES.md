## 3. SATURATION & CHAOS FIELD PROMPTS
1. "What fundamental operational assumption would break this entire system model if proven false?"
2. "What is the rarest, most catastrophic real-world event that occurs here once a year?"
3. "If the most experienced operator were replaced by a malicious or incompetent worker, how does the system fail?"
4. "What environmental or volumetric extremes have we failed to observe in person?"
5. "What secondary operational feedback loops could quietly compound an unnoticed data error?"
6. "How could an operator and counterparty collude to systematically defeat this proposed workflow?"
7. "If grid power, cellular connectivity, and local hardware fail simultaneously, what is the recovery path?"
8. "What specific operational detail have we dismissed as an anomaly that might represent a structural flaw?"
9. "How would an insider manipulate this data contract to evade audit detection?"
10. "Can we identify three consecutive interviews that added zero new failure modes to the ledger?"

## 4. RESEARCHER FAILURE MODES
- **Exhaustion-Driven Closure:** Declaring research complete simply because the researcher is fatigued.
- **Anchoring on the Golden Path:** Focusing on smooth, nominal workflows and treating edge cases as rare anomalies.
- **Consensus Bias:** Dismissing dissenting perspectives from low-ranking helpers, drivers, or cleaners.
- **Checklist Fallacy:** Confusing completing an interview questionnaire with achieving conceptual saturation.
