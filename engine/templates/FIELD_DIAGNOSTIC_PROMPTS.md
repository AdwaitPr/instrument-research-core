# USE: Extract during physical Gemba interviews. Organized by interviewee tier.

## TIER 1: THE OPERATOR / FLOOR TECHNICIAN (Ground Execution)
1. "Show me the last time a transaction went wrong—what broke physically, and how did you resolve it?"
2. "When the queue is out the door, what is the very first step you skip or shortcut to keep the line moving?"
3. "Show me where you write down numbers when the computer or printer is slow or frozen."
4. "How do you handle data entry when your hands are covered in grease, water, or dust?"
5. "If a driver or counterparty is shouting at you over an error, what makes them concede the argument?"

## TIER 2: THE MUNIM / CLERK / ACCOUNTANT (Shadow Records)
1. "Where are the actual daily notes kept before they are keyed into the official accounting software?"
2. "What is the most frequent reason physical cash in the drawer does not match the ledger at closing?"
3. "How do you record transactions for long-time trusted parties who do not receive a formal slip today?"
4. "Show me how you adjust for material loss, moisture cuts, or unrecorded damages in the formal books."
5. "What happens when an official receipt needs to be altered or canceled after the counterparty has departed?"

## TIER 3: THE HELPER / DRIVER / TRANSPORTER (Adversarial Counterparty)
1. "How do you confirm that you aren't being cheated on the weight, count, or grading at this counter?"
2. "What is the standard procedure if you arrive after formal closing hours and need to drop a load?"
3. "What informal tips, speed money, or unloading fees must be paid in cash to clear this checkpoint?"
4. "What is the easiest way someone could skim material or fake a delivery chit at this yard?"
5. "When a dispute over an order happens, who makes the final call, and how long does it take?"
